## Overview

TechRunners is a 2.5D open-world MMORPG set in the cyberpunk city of New Haven. Players build their loadouts, explore the city, compete in PvP, join crews, and fight for territory in a persistent online world.

## End-to-end development

I worked across the complete development lifecycle rather than on a single isolated feature. My contribution spans the Unity client, multiplayer gameplay and progression systems, interface implementation, backend-connected features, content integration, platform preparation, testing, and the work required to move the game toward release.

This breadth makes TechRunners the most complete demonstration of my experience building and shipping a connected game: turning a large game concept into a cohesive, playable product prepared for a public Steam launch.
